<section id="learning" class="section--first">
  <h2>Learning About Open Source Design</h2>
  <p class="section__intro">
    Understanding how design works in open source communities starts with learning from those already doing it. We gather talks, guides, and real case studies from designers working in the open source space.
  </p>
  <p class="section__text">
    Whether you're new to open source or a seasoned contributor, you'll find practical resources here: talks from major conferences like FOSDEM, community guides on contribution pathways, and patterns that have worked for real projects. All curated with an eye toward what actually applies to your work.
  </p>
  <p class="section__text">
    The goal is simple: share what works. No theory divorced from practice, no gatekeeping. Just signal—the good stuff worth your time.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="participating">
  <h2>Participating in Open Source Design</h2>
  <p class="section__intro">
    There are many ways to contribute to open source as a designer. Whether you're looking to sponsor your time to a specific project, help design a feature, review information architecture, or just collaborate async with a global team.
  </p>
  <p class="section__text">
    The barrier to entry is often unclear. We're working to change that. By publishing design briefs, hosting collaboration calls, and sharing job postings for designers in open source, we aim to make the path to contribution clearer.
  </p>
</section>
